Add MusicPlayer with a compact cassette opener, responsive cassette controls, externally supplied audio URLs or files, sequential and shuffle playback, history navigation, volume, seeking, and optional button feedback. Shuffle is disabled for empty and single-track playlists; audio loads only for the selected track after manual playback starts. Theme colors, translated labels, reduced motion, and independent instances are supported.
